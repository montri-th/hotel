# CityMETER Hotel — owner preferences

Set 2026-11-01 (release 0.1.0) on the owner request “จากที่เราทำ demo ของ CityMETER: Luxury brands มาแล้ว … ขอให้ทำแบบเดียวกันให้กับ CityMETER: Hotel แล้ว publish ที่ github.com/montri-th/hotel โดยใช้ข้อมูลจาก [the hotel research master] และ dataset อื่นที่ผูกกับ registry เพื่อ Leverage มาปรับปรุง value to ICP's benefit ตาม product statement ให้ดีที่สุด”, and the owner's answer the same day to publish “รายโรงแรม รวมราคาที่บันทึก” (per hotel, including the recorded prices). Carried over from CityMETER · Luxury Brands 0.5, Restaurants 0.5, Pharmacy 0.1.1 and Fitness 0.3.1 where the same contract applies (code base: montri-th/fitness @50ec2b6).

Release 0.2.0 (2026-11-01) adds the owner's answers to the five-point consult (“ทำทุกข้อตามที่พี่แนะนำเลยครับ”): terrain for resorts (point 2), symbols sized by rooms (point 4), names and chain logos near the middle of the view, “เน้นให้แสดงโรงแรมที่สำคัญก่อน คือราคาสูงและจำนวนห้องมากก่อน” (point 5), and “ใช้ symbol, icon แทน text ยาว ๆ โดยเฉพาะป้ายใน map”. Point 3 (buildings per อปท., source per area by the 15 % rule, เขต in Bangkok, research boundaries for the top resorts and pool villas) is approved for the next release and not in 0.2.0.

- Apply current LDS 0.9.7 (v0.9.7-owner.1, standalone-0.9.7-r1, color-srgb-10) and the Location Intelligence Profile for LDS 0.9.7 (`li.supply` for rooms), and preserve the map-first CityMETER layout. Colours come only from `assets/theme.css`, `js/scales.js` and `js/cues.js`; never approximate a value by eye. Analytical colours are the same HEX on both themes; EVID-05 map treatments use the `dataState` tokens (`--data-no-data`, `--data-zero-outline`). `npm run check:lds -- --lds <base> --li <profile>` compares every shipped value with both source files.
- Read `PRODUCT_STATEMENT.md` before changing what the first screen argues. The question is “ห้องพักกระจุกอยู่ตรงไหน และใกล้รถไฟฟ้าแค่ไหน”.
- Measure is a contract (`js/model.js#METRICS`, `rowValue`): rooms recorded (Σ `rooms_selected`, “ห้อง”, `li.supply`) by default, records (“แห่ง”, `count`) on the switch; the map, legend, labels, Aha, ranks and charts read the same measure. Unknown rooms are never 0: a record with unknown rooms is counted apart, a district whose records all have unknown rooms is `no_data` (hatch), and a chip or bar whose rooms are unknown reads in records (“2 แห่ง”). Never call a figure occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, revenue, demand, a market share, a market size, a density or rooms available per night.
- Recorded prices (CityMETER Hotel V3, `price_period = unknown`) are published per hotel only (owner 2026-11-01), always as “ราคาที่บันทึก” with “ไม่ทราบช่วงเวลา” in the hotel panel; never summed, averaged across hotels or called a rate. Star ratings are not published.
- Aha first, in 3D: the first paint is the `city` preset framing up to three เขต/อำเภอ with the most of the measure in the province that holds the most of it at district level, focused automatically (`auto`, no `areas` param). Show the share (Σ focus ÷ the province's placed total), numerator and denominator, the province's total placed only at province level, the rail comparison (share within 500 m of an operating station: focus vs the rest of the province, rows with a point, both bases; hidden when nothing in the province is near an operating station) and the district(s) with the most outside that province (“มากกว่าเขตใดใน…” when it exceeds the focus), then the actions (ดูทุกพื้นที่, เปรียบเทียบพื้นที่). Never cut through a tie, never focus every district that holds the measure (N ≤ 3 → top N − 1; `js/area-insight.js`). Snapshot numbers in the handoff JSON are fixtures, never UI constants.
- Membership is the district matched at build time from the row's own data (`row.admin`: source · name · point · province · conflict · none). Never re-assign by point-in-polygon on display coordinates or by a hotel name at runtime. Rows without a district stay in every total and are named apart (“ระบุได้แค่จังหวัด”).
- Context comes from datasets of the same registry, joined by id with the commit and SHA-256 recorded (`meta.context`): CityMETER · Transit stations (operating drawn as quiet clickable rings; under construction named in words only, “โครงการ ไม่ใช่บริการ”), CityMETER Shopping centers venues (linked by id to the venue's page), research building candidates (industry and public brand only, “อาคารเดียวกันตามงานวิจัย”, never a located-in claim). Every distance is a straight line between reference points (“ระยะตรง”), never a walking route, a travel time or a catchment.
- Comparison set (`js/profile-model.js#compsetOf`): same accommodation group, known rooms ½–2× the hotel's own, within 2 km straight line, nearest first, every row of the dataset; always worded as “รายชื่อเริ่มต้นสำหรับเทียบ ไม่ใช่คู่แข่งที่ยืนยันแล้ว”, with the total supply round the hotel beside it.
- Groups (chains) join brand → `17_BRANDS` → group by id only; a brand without a group reads “ไม่ระบุเครือ”; never infer a group from a name. A record without a brand is not proof of an independent hotel. Link changes are made in the research sheet itself, then re-exported and rebuilt — never patched in `data/`.
- National scale: `data/area-index.json` has every district without geometry; outlines load per province (`js/frames.js`) for the selection and for the provinces under the map at district zoom (≤ 6 per move). Never load all 77 frame files up front.
- Focus + quiet context: detail (fills, coloured cue symbols, rounded focus massing) only for the focused part. Everything else stays visible but quiet. Below zoom 8 the province frames carry the reading — except with a จังหวัด filter.
- Accommodation groups bind by stable id to LDS series with the series shape cue (`js/cues.js#GROUP_SERIES`; DATAVIZ-05). Never colour a category by hue alone. No star cue. Symbol size (owner 2026-11-01): the symbol's area grows with the recorded rooms (`js/model.js#pinRadius`, `PIN_SCALE`, cap `ROOMS_SIZE_CAP`), the colour stays the group's; unknown rooms are an open (hollow) shape at the 50-room size, never a small filled mark; quiet context dots follow the same rule at about half size, capped at `CONTEXT_MAX_RADIUS`; larger symbols are drawn first. The legend key shows 50 · 200 · 800 ห้อง and the hollow mark. Ordinal bars (size bands, stations, groups) use one neutral foundation fill.
- Names near the middle of the view (`js/labels.js`, owner 2026-11-01): from zoom 12.5, focus rows in the central ellipse, ranked by `row.importance` (mean of the national percentiles of recorded rooms and recorded price — avg, else max, else min; a missing price is never guessed; ranking only, never shown), at most 9 on desktop and 5 on a phone; measured placement that never covers the chrome, the district chips, the popup or another label. A label is text, never a logo (owner 2026-11-01: “ป้าย logo เล็กไปก็มองไม่ออก … หาแบบที่ user อ่านง่าย เห็นเข้าใจเร็ว”): the group cue, then for a branded stay the brand in bold over the site (its name without the brand, type and place words), otherwise one short name, and the bed pictogram with rooms; the full name is the accessible name. Hover or focus opens a card with the chain logo at a readable size (≈ 40 px tall) and icon chips (type, rooms, recorded price, brand, terrain); no caveats in the card.
- Chain-brand logos (`js/logos.js`, `data/brand-logos.json`, `scripts/build-logos.py`): the research team's logo library, every file SHA-256 checked against its manifest and `48_LOGO_LIBRARY`; identity marks, never recoloured, traced or redrawn into the icon system; theme-invariant backing; shown only where they can be read — the hover card (≈ 40 px tall) and the hotel panel (≈ 44 px) — never shrunk into a map label; the brand or hotel name always stays beside the logo or in the control's name. A brand without an accepted logo shows its name.
- Terrain (`js/terrain.js`, owner 2026-11-01 point 2): terrain mode switches on by itself at site scale (zoom ≥ 12.5, 3D view, not a building or stay being looked at) where the visible ground spans ≥ 30 m: 3D ground × 1.5 (said in the legend) and a quiet hillshade in basemap tones; the district fill then switches off at 12.5 — a hard switch, never a fade. Flat cities load no terrain. Terrain facts of a stay (ground elevation, relief within 500 m, higher/lower than the ring, sea within a ring, OSM tree cover > 0) are facts of the ground, never a view, price or risk; unknown is left out, never 0; the panel's provenance line names SRTM 30 m via Terrain Tiles on AWS and OpenStreetMap.
- Map text is icon-first (owner 2026-11-01): district chips carry the measure's pictogram (bed = ห้อง, hotel = แห่ง), the status line is a pin and a number, legend items are a symbol and one or two words; full sentences live in titles and accessible names. Icon-only controls only where the symbol is conventional (LDS ICON-01); pictograms come from the approved subset (`scripts/build-pictogram-font.py`).
- Outbound actions (`js/ask.js`): ถาม Google AI (AI Mode, `udm=50`) with a question written for the view, Google Maps search, and Street View only where the view has a point (“ใกล้สถานี” for a station's reference point). Public names and published points only, https, new tab with `noopener`; one line says the answers are Google's, not CityMETER data.
- Building view and MapLibre: follow the Fitness 0.3.1 contracts unchanged (`js/camera.js`, `js/massing.js`, `js/venue-xray.js`, `js/maplibre-guard.js` with MapLibre pinned at 5.6.2, `GEOJSON_MAXZOOM`). Do not reorder the massing, x-ray, station and pin layers casually.
- One symbol always represents one row with a point; never cluster or aggregate. HTML pins from zoom 15.5 inside the focus only (≤ 320); quiet context dots are never focusable or clickable.
- Breadcrumb is ประเทศไทย › จังหวัด › เขต/อำเภอ with production codes and deep links `/v3/citymeter/{code}/D/{district}?d=hotel` (dataset code `hotel` in the production bundle, checked 2026-11-01). A hotel or a station is the panel heading, never a crumb.
- Every panel view ends with exactly one provenance line — dataset · source · date · boundary — and one clause on what the counted unit is (`provenance()` in `js/panel.js`); rail and retail sources join that line when the view shows them. Keep other caveats out of popups, tooltips and lists (owner, Restaurants 0.4.0: “caveats … เอาให้เนียน นึกถึง user benefit เป็นหลัก”).
- Copy: short, natural Thai — ห้อง / แห่ง / ที่พัก / ประเภท / ขนาด / เครือ / แบรนด์ / สถานี / เขต·อำเภอ / ระยะตรง / ราคาที่บันทึก; lead with pictures (pictogram, cue, fact chips); keep sources, methods and ids behind one “แหล่ง พื้นที่ และพิกัด” disclosure; units on every number; a number and its unit never split across lines (`nw` in `js/panel.js`). No static counts in page copy (help, meta): numbers come from the data.
- Charts: matrix cells are equal in size; district bars carry the map's ramp colour. No CSV download. No decorative brackets or coloured left rails. Visible focus everywhere.
- Privacy and publication: never copy addresses, phone numbers, raw coordinates, operator names, record ids of the sources, researcher notes, quality flags, README text or raw source records (`meta.excludedFields`); source links are public https pages only, never the production API. Never commit the internal research sheet or its export, private Drive/Sheet URLs, credentials, the full LDS library or the Location Intelligence Profile. Never put Landometer prices, contracts, proposals or pipeline in this repository. Review Thai at desktop and 390 px in light and dark before claiming done; local renders are not proof of a live release — run `npm run verify:live` and walk the live site.
- Releases bump one cache tag `?v=YYYYMMDD-rN` on every entry in `index.html` and, through the import map there, on every ES module, together with `package.json` version, `design-intent.json` and the handoff JSON `productVersion` (`tests/release.test.js`). A new module must be added to the import map. Icon fonts inside the stylesheets carry `?v=<first 8 hex of their SHA-256>` (`tests/icons.test.js`).

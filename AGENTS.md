- Building view and MapLibre: follow the Fitness 0.3.1 contracts (`js/shell/camera.js`, `js/massing.js`, `js/shell/venue-xray.js`, `js/shell/maplibre-guard.js` with MapLibre pinned at 5.6.2, `GEOJSON_MAXZOOM`). On terrain (0.3.0, owner “อยากให้เห็นอาคารบนภูมิประเทศ”) the building stands on the drawn ground: `fitBuilding` computes with the centre's ground elevation in every jump (the landed, ground-clamped camera) and pans on the anchor's ground plane (never `map.unproject`, whose terrain ray reads the last frame); `projectPoint` adds the ground under a point; the x-ray roof is lifted by the ground at each piece's centroid, as MapLibre lifts fill-extrusions, and its walls go down to the lowest ground under the piece's outline (0.3.2; a fixed depth showed through the slope); the fit frames each piece's base at that lowest ground and its roof at the centroid's (the fourth value of a fit point); the frame is refitted when the ground under the building changes by ≥ 3 m (DEM tiles arriving), a new building arrives or the free box changes once the camera is still (the legend re-rendered for the new zoom, trays, the phone sheet: `watchFreeBox`, 0.3.2), within three refits (`MAX_REFITS`), never after a user camera move. MapLibre 5.6.2 leaves the centre's elevation frozen after a programmatic ease on terrain; `guardElevationFreeze` lands it on the ground after every ease (0.3.2, pinned to 5.6.2) and `fitBuilding` damps its landed rounds (half steps), so the framed building does not drift. The building fit uses a shift lens (0.3.2, `lensPadding`): padding moves the principal point up to the free box's centre — never below the canvas centre, so the horizon stays out — and the camera looks straight at the building at the eye pitch on a phone as on a desktop (zero padding left a phone's strip above the sheet on a grazing ray where the fit did not converge). It computes with MapLibre's camera lift off (`withoutLift`, `Map#_elevateCameraIfInsideTerrain`) and asks MapLibre's own check about the camera it found: another corner of the building when that camera would stand inside the terrain, else the pitch MapLibre allows; the target carries its pitch and the centre's elevation.
- Stay → building (0.3.2, owner “… 2-point perspective ของ 3d building mass ทุกกรณี”): choosing a stay with a point — a pin, a label, a list row or search (`selectHotel`) — switches the map to 3D and opens the eye (two-point) camera on a building (a link does the same unless it asks for the plan, `view3d=0`): the drawn building whose outline holds the pin (`joinBuildings` → `byRow`, legend “อาคารของที่พัก”), else the drawn building nearest the pin within 300 m (`nearestBuilding`: distance to the outline, the largest piece within 10 m of the nearest; legend “อาคารใกล้หมุด”, framed one zoom step wider with the pin in the frame). If nothing is drawn within 300 m the buildings are loaded once more for 800 m round the pin (`HOTEL_REACH_M`, `HOTEL_REACH_FAR_M`). The nearest building is a camera choice only — never a join, a count, a “ในอาคารนี้” list or a located-in claim. A stay without a point keeps its area view; where no building is drawn within 800 m the two-point camera frames the ground round the pin.

Opening the stay highlights, x-rays and frames (two-point, with the stay's pin in the frame) every building whose drawn outline holds one of the points (`js/shell/building-join.js#buildingsAtPoints`; a point no outline holds is left out, never snapped), the massing square round the pin widened to hold the site (`siteReach`). A site is a grouping of buildings for the map, never a land title, a title area or ownership.
